# Privacy Policy for Bedside Alarm Clock

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

Bedside Alarm Clock is designed primarily as a local, on-device alarm clock application. Most information used by the app remains on your Android device.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Bedside Alarm Clock stores information needed to provide its features locally on your device. This may include:

- Alarm times, enabled states, snooze settings, and alarm sound selections
- Display preferences, colors, clock settings, and other app preferences
- Sleep Aid settings and selected sounds
- YouTube video IDs and titles when YouTube is selected as an alarm source
- Imported local audio and information about those audio files
- Alarm, playback, Sleep Aid, and application runtime state
- Local diagnostic and history information used to troubleshoot alarm and playback behavior

Imported audio is copied into the app's private storage for playback.

Bedside Alarm Clock does not operate a developer-owned server or account system to collect this information.

## YouTube

Bedside Alarm Clock optionally allows you to select a YouTube video as an alarm audio source.

When you use YouTube functionality, the app uses an embedded YouTube player and may request YouTube content or related resources over the Internet. Information such as the requested video, playback activity, IP address, device or browser information, cookies, or other identifiers may be transmitted to or processed by YouTube or Google as part of providing that service.

Google and YouTube process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Bedside Alarm Clock does not control the information independently collected, retained, or processed by those services.

You do not need to use YouTube as an alarm source; other alarm-audio options are available.

## Local Audio

You may select audio files from your device for use as alarm sounds or Sleep Aid audio. Selected audio may be copied into Bedside Alarm Clock's private app storage.

Bedside Alarm Clock does not upload your imported audio to a developer-operated server.

## Diagnostics and History

Bedside Alarm Clock maintains local diagnostic and history information to help diagnose alarm reliability, playback, and application behavior.

This information may include alarm and snooze events, playback state, process information, app and Android versions, device model, battery and charging state, connectivity observations, and performance information.

Diagnostic information remains local to the device unless you explicitly copy or export it. Diagnostic reports may be copied to the Android clipboard at your request.

The app does not automatically send diagnostic or crash information to the developer.

## Backups and Exports

Bedside Alarm Clock allows you to explicitly export settings and portable backups using Android's file picker. A portable backup may include app settings and, when selected, imported local audio.

You choose where exported files are stored. The app does not automatically upload these exports to a developer-operated server.

Android system backup or device-transfer features may also back up eligible app data according to your Android device and backup settings. Those services are controlled by Android and the backup provider.

## Permissions and Android System Access

Bedside Alarm Clock uses Android permissions and system services needed for features such as:

- Scheduling exact alarms
- Displaying alarm and Sleep Aid notifications
- Presenting full-screen alarms
- Running alarm and media-playback foreground services
- Checking and requesting battery-optimization settings needed for reliable alarms
- Reading relevant Do Not Disturb policy state to determine whether alarm or media audio is permitted
- Accessing the Internet when network-based features such as YouTube are used

These system capabilities are used to provide app functionality and are not used by the developer to create a profile about you.

## Information Bedside Alarm Clock Does Not Request

Bedside Alarm Clock does not require an app account and does not request access to your:

- Location
- Contacts
- Calendar
- Camera
- Microphone for audio recording
- Call logs or SMS messages
- Health or fitness information
- Financial or payment information

The app does not contain advertising SDKs or developer-operated analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services.

## User-Controlled Sharing

Any settings or audio backup that you export is created only at your request and saved to a location you select through Android.

If you copy a diagnostic report, it is placed on the Android clipboard. Clipboard contents may be accessible to Android or other applications according to Android's clipboard behavior.

## Data Deletion

You can remove Bedside Alarm Clock's locally stored application data using Android's app-storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Files that you previously exported to locations outside the app must be deleted separately from those locations.

Data independently processed by Google, YouTube, Android backup services, or other services is subject to the policies and controls of those services.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Bedside Alarm Clock's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please use the contact information provided for Bedside Alarm Clock in its Google Play listing.
